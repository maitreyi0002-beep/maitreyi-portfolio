# Maitreyi | Product designer

## There’s a person behind that click...
I’m Maitreyi, a product designer curious about the person behind the screen. I look at how people think, what makes them hesitate, and what helps them feel confident.

I work across AI products, design systems, and code. I enjoy turning an ambiguous problem into something clear and useful, staying close from the first question to the final build.

## Selected work

### Super Agent
Four AI agents, one conversation. Helping people get things done without choosing which assistant to ask.
[Read case study](https://maitreyi0002-beep.github.io/maitreyi-portfolio/work/super-agent/)

### Connect

## Latest experiments
[components.codes](https://components.codes/): A growing library of considered interactions. Live React + Tailwind components, with the code ready to make your own.
